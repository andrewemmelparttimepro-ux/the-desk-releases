Already have The Desk?

On Windows or Mac, choose Check for updates inside the app. If your older copy has no update option, install the current version once from this page. Keep your existing app data; your business connection remains saved.

On iPhone and iPad, open The Desk in TestFlight or the App Store and choose Update. The Desk's Settings can take you to the right page.
